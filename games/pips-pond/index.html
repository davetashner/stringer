How it works

Pick a number size on the home screen: Tadpole (0–5), Froglet (0–10), Frog (0–20), Bullfrog (0–50) or Pond King (0–100). Subtraction never goes below zero. Stars from every game unlock stickers and hats for Pip. Pond Race adjusts the duck's speed after each race so it stays a close contest.

Play full screen on iPad

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Progress is saved on this device only.
