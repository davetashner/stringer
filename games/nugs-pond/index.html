How it works

Pick a number size on the home screen: Tadpole (0–5), Froglet (0–10), Frog (0–20), Bullfrog (0–50) or Pond King (0–100). Every game mixes adding and taking away, and subtraction never goes below zero. Stars from every game unlock stickers and hats for Nug. Pond Race adjusts the duck's speed after each race so it stays a close contest. Fast wrong guesses are discouraged: the first gets a reminder to slow down, and a second soon after pauses the game for a few seconds while the clock keeps running. Bubble Pop allows one tap per question, Pond Race two tries, and Fish Friends three tries before the answer is shown.
